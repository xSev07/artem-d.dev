I have used the Six Jars budgeting method for more than 10 years. Thanks to it, I do not have to keep wondering whether I can afford a new board game or go to a cafe, whether I will have enough money for a vacation, or whether I will be able to pay the rent on time if my paycheck is delayed.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to follow the traditional Six Jars allocation exactly?

No. The traditional allocation is a ready-made starting point, not a fixed set of rules.

Can I customize the app to suit my needs?

Yes. You can rename the six default jars, remove jars you do not need, and choose different percentages. You can also create additional jars if needed.

Do I need to record every purchase separately?

No. You can combine several purchases from the same jar into a single entry. For example, you do not have to record every supermarket purchase separately—you can simply enter the total amount spent on groceries.

What if I cannot afford to save right now?

You decide how much money to put toward savings. You can start with a small amount or, for now, allocate your entire salary to current expenses—the app does not require you to save a fixed percentage.

How is this different from a regular expense tracker?

Regular expense trackers mainly help you record purchases and see where your money has already gone. 6 Jars Finance automatically divides your salary between the spending and saving categories you set up in advance, so you can always see how much is available in each jar without dipping into money set aside for a vacation, a major purchase, or another goal.
